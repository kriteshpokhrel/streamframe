StreamFrame

Local-first highlight clipping and short-form publishing.

StreamFrame is a local desktop tool that turns a live stream or a video file into vertical short clips, then publishes them to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It runs on the operator's machine, not as a hosted service.

The tool is operated by an individual creator for their own channels. It is not a public product and does not accept sign-ups or store data about other people.
